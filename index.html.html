01 / The person behind the analysis

About me

A networking foundation.
A focus on defensive security.

I am Mutiu Olaoluwa Salaudeen, a cybersecurity professional with a background in networking and cybersecurity, focused on security monitoring, threat detection, log analysis, vulnerability assessment, and incident investigation.

My professional interests center on identifying potential security weaknesses, examining suspicious activities, analyzing network communications, and understanding security events that may indicate malicious or unauthorized activity.

I work with cybersecurity tools and practical lab environments to develop and demonstrate skills in network traffic analysis, vulnerability scanning, authentication log investigation, and security reporting. I approach security investigations methodically by examining available evidence, identifying relevant indicators, documenting observations, and recommending appropriate defensive measures.

My technical interests include Security Operations Center (SOC) workflows, Windows and Linux security logs, SIEM technologies, network security, and vulnerability management. I am particularly interested in how security teams collect and analyze events, distinguish suspicious behavior from legitimate activity, prioritize risks, and respond to potential threats.

Through documented cybersecurity projects and lab-based investigations, I aim to demonstrate my technical reasoning, analytical approach, problem-solving ability, and commitment to continuous professional development.

I am pursuing opportunities in Security Operations, Security Analysis, and Vulnerability Assessment where I can contribute to security monitoring, investigation, risk identification, and the improvement of organizational security practices.
